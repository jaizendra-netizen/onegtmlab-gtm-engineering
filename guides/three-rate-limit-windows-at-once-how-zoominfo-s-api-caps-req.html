Three rate-limit windows at once: how ZoomInfo's API caps requests

Most rate limits are one number. ZoomInfo's GTM AI API runs three at the same time: a per-second cap, a sliding per-hour cap and a sliding per-day cap. Your code is only as fast as the tightest of the three at any moment, so it pays to understand how they fit together. This guide is drawn from the ZoomInfo API review.

The three windows

The limits are per tenant, in three simultaneous windows (per-second, sliding per-hour and sliding per-day). The page lists tiers:

  • Builder: 5 requests a second, 10,800 an hour, 129,600 a day.
  • Standard (default): 25 a second, 54,000 an hour, 648,000 a day.
  • Scaling: 35 a second, 75,600 an hour, 907,200 a day.
  • Data Distribution partner: 50 a second by default.
  • GTM AI Free tier: 5 a second, with 5 apps (gtm.ai/pricing).

A credit-usage page variant lists a default of 25 a second, with Premium add-on at 30 and Premium+ add-on at 35. The page flags that the two sources differ, so check your own account.

Why three windows matter

A burst can hit the per-second cap while the hour has plenty left. A steady job can drain the hour while the second looks fine. Treat each as its own bucket and design your client to respect all three.

Helpful details from the page: rejected requests do not consume quota. Headers come in three families, X-RateLimit-Limit-, Remaining- and Reset- by Second, Hour and Day. On a 429 you also get Retry-After, X-RateLimit-Rejected-Bucket and X-RateLimit-Reset, and the rejected-bucket header tells you which window you hit. The legacy X-RateLimit-Limit and Remaining headers are removed on 2027-01-01, so read the new ones now.

Page sizes are limits too

Search returns up to 25 records per request via API and 10 via MCP and CLI. Enrich takes up to 25 records per API call and up to 10 per MCP call. The page lists "large batch enrichment in one call" among the limits, since 25 per API request is the cap.

What a call costs

Search, lookup, find similar and recommended contacts are free per call. Enrichment of a company or contact, or unlocking an intent record, costs 1 data credit per new record, with free re-enrich for 12 months under Records Under Management. A data credit is $0.38 at the $1,000 spend tier, and an AI credit is $0.05, with the pricing page citing about 1 to 20 credits per AI action and account research about 9 to 12. Loading a record into a GTM Studio audience costs 1 data credit per new matched record, with unmatched own-data rows free.

Plans

GTM AI Free gives 1,000 data and 1,000 AI credits at signup with no credit card, plus MCP, API and CLI access for 5 apps, and audiences of 5 x 500 rows. Top-ups start from $20, and 5,000 credits are listed at $1,900 before a 70% first-purchase promo, $570 after. GTM AI Enterprise is a custom quote with annual volume, bonus credits, Records Under Management, unlimited apps, custom rate limits, an audience waterfall (25+ vendors), mobile phone data, SSO or SAML and SLA support. The classic Sales, Copilot, Marketing and ABM plans are quote only.

Getting credentials

ZoomInfo uses OAuth. Enterprise customers create a Standard App in the Developer Portal (developer.zoominfo.com), and GTM.ai self-serve customers use the GTM Studio Developer tab. Client credentials need an integration user. Tokens come from POST https://api.zoominfo.com/gtm/oauth/v1/token, with scopes such as api:data:contact, api:data:company, api:data:intent, api:data:news and api:data:scoops. A test bearer token can be generated in the portal, but it makes real production calls and consumes credits.

A client design

  1. Keep a token bucket for each of the three windows.
  2. Read the Remaining headers after each response.
  3. On a 429, read X-RateLimit-Rejected-Bucket and back off by the matching window.
  4. Prefer free search to narrow before you pay to enrich.

The page lists 34 endpoints and an official MCP server, and recommends the API for free search, lookup and lookalikes before paying, with credits only on enrich. The ZoomInfo API review covering three-window rate limits and credit prices has the full table, verified on 25 Oct 2026.
